# Consolidated Sensei Debrief Helper (Updated 13-Belt Path)



## Sensei Debrief Checklist

1. **Introduce yourself** to the parent.


2. **Mention the name of the project/game** and describe how it works.


3. **Discuss the coding Category/Label and Specific Concepts** the Ninja learned and practiced.


4. **Highlight problem-solving:** If there were bugs or challenges, talk about how the Ninja overcame them or what they will focus on next time.


5. **Share belt progress:** Let the parents know where they are in the curriculum (e.g., *"Ninja Alex just finished Level 2 of Yellow Belt—they're halfway to Orange!"*).


6. **Wrap up with praise:** Share a fun tidbit from the session or something specific they did particularly well.
